Good to know.

Are you really open until 2am?

Yes, every night except Wednesday. Doors open at 4:30pm and the kitchen runs until 2am.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes. The vegan and vegetarian section includes original, tomato and mala spicy soups on a shiitake mushroom broth, plus General Tao's tofu. Vegetable versions of the ramen, udon, fried rice, hibachi and sushi rolls are on the menu too. Ask us which ones are fully vegan.

Is anything gluten-free?

Some dishes are marked GF on the menu. If you have celiac disease or a serious allergy, tell us when you order and we'll walk you through what's safe for you.

Can you fit a big group?

The dining room is small, but we've seated big groups before. Call (802) 540-1318 ahead so we can get ready.

Where do I park?

There's street parking along North Winooski Avenue and on the side streets around us.
